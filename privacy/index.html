Molecular Biology is Hard

Privacy policy

November 22, 2026

Molecular Studios makes Molecular Biology is Hard (MBiH) for iPhone and iPad. You can play without making an MBiH account. Your lab and notebook are saved on your device.

Your saved lab

MBiH stores your lab setup, specimens, notes, experiment results, and preferences locally to reopen your work. The optional Ed’s Desk widget reads a local summary. Device backups may include app data according to your system settings.

If you export a notebook or recovery file, your device asks where to send it. You choose whether to share it. The public app has no in-app advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

Reminders and Apple services

MBiH schedules lab reminders on your device when you allow notifications. You can change that permission in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Your notification settings control what appears on your lock screen.

If you use Game Center, MBiH reports earned achievement IDs to Apple. Apple processes optional cosmetic purchases and restores through the App Store. MBiH uses purchase entitlement information to unlock those items. Apple handles payment details.

When you email support

Email is optional. If you contact us, we receive your email address, the message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer you, diagnose the issue, and keep track of related follow-up.

Access is limited to those handling support for Molecular Studios and email providers that deliver, store, and protect the messages. Those providers are expected to protect the data they handle. We keep support messages and attachments only as long as needed to resolve the request and follow-up, or to meet legal, security, or dispute-resolution obligations.

Internal beta builds may offer an opt-in log stored on the device. Recording can be turned off and the log can be cleared in Beta feedback. We receive a copy only if you choose to send it to us.

Your choices and deletion requests

To remove the local app data from a device, delete the app. Copies you exported or backed up are managed separately through the service or destination you chose. Manage Game Center and purchase information through Apple.

To ask about your data or request deletion of support messages and attachments we hold, email Solarisdreamer@outlook.com. We may ask you to confirm the address that sent the original request so we can protect your information.

This website and paper links

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. Websites you open from the MBiH Library have their own privacy practices and do not receive your saved laboratory from MBiH.
